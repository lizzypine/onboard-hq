# Dental license

The internal checklist calls for review of CODA-accredited dental school, NBDE Parts I and II, and ADEX records against the current application instructions. Confirm which documents the issuing authority must send and which qualifications apply to this applicant.

In MyPVL, select My Applications, Apply for New License, Board of Dentistry, then the appropriate Dentist license type. Complete the required PDF and submit supporting documents through the licensing portal. Authorize the designated onboarding coordinator to inquire about the application. The contact has to be confirmed.

# Hawaii NED and HI-PDMP

After the Hawaii dental license, prepare the HI-PDMP and eHawaii accounts and NED application with the licensing contact. The oral-code request depends on Manager-reviewed applicability. The earlier internal checklist described a six-day expiry and recreating a PDMP account. This conflicts with official guidance for existing accounts. The recovery procedure requires review.

# Federal DEA registration

After Hawaii controlled-substance registration, prepare the federal DEA registration. Use the main office for the business address and the applicant’s own home for the mailing address. The guide does not provide either address or a list of prescribing locations. Obtain current approved prescribing-site information from the Manager.

# Final handoff

Send completed license copies to the credentialing specialist and human resources. The separate handoff of the DEA certificate and any approved oral code goes to pharmacy leadership under the Manager-approved procedure. Confirm the current contact roles before distribution. The earlier instruction “include my name” does not identify an approved recipient.
